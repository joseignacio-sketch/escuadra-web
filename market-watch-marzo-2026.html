Key Findings

March 2026 Highlights

01

El Tezal-West leads in volume

Most units sold with 20 transactions at an average price of US$399K and US$3,137/sqm — the most active submarket this month.

02

Palmilla Ocean Side tops dollar volume

3 sales totaling US$21.5M with an average of US$17,549/sqm — the highest price per sqm recorded this month.

03

20 transactions above US$1M

A quarter of all sales surpassed the million-dollar mark, reflecting continued strength in the luxury segment.

04

Under-$1M condos: stable pricing

43 condo sales at US$3,720/sqm — 2% below the 12-month average, signaling relative stability in the accessible segment.

05

Under-$1M houses: price surge

17 house sales at US$3,034/sqm — 19% above the 12-month average (US$2,539), the sharpest monthly increase this year.

06

Inventory continues rising

Full market: Houses 23.0 months (+0.8) / Condos 26.5 months (+1.0). Buyer-favorable conditions persist across all segments.
